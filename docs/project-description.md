# CropTrader: A Web-Based Marketplace Connecting Farmers Directly with Consumers

## 1. Problem
Farmers often sell their products through several intermediaries, such as brokers, wholesalers and retailers, before the products reach the consumer. Each step adds its own cost; a study covering 61 countries found that farmers receive on average only about 27% of what consumers spend on food eaten at home (Yi et al., 2021). Small-scale farmers in particular rarely have a sales channel of their own, and individual consumers who want to buy directly from producers have few easy ways to find them.

## 2. Why This Problem Matters
This gap affects both sides. Farmers earn less from their work, which makes small-scale farming harder to sustain, while consumers pay more and often receive products that have spent several days in the supply chain. A direct channel can make prices more transparent, give consumers access to fresher products and support local production. Since more and more people already shop online, a simple digital marketplace is a realistic way to bring these two groups together.

## 3. Target Users
- **Farmers:** small and medium-sized producers who want to list their products, set their own prices and manage incoming orders.
- **Consumers:** individuals who want to buy fresh agricultural products directly from producers, compare what is available and order the quantity they need.

## 4. Main Objective
The main objective of CropTrader is to develop a web-based marketplace where farmers can offer their products directly to individual consumers. Farmers will create product listings, and consumers will browse these listings and place orders. Order and stock management features will make the buying process more organized and transparent for both sides.

## 5. Main Features
- Registration and login with two roles: farmer and consumer
- Creating, editing and removing product listings with price, available quantity and location
- Browsing and searching product listings
- Placing an order directly from a listing
- Automatic stock update after each order
- Order status updates by the farmer (pending, preparing, delivered)
- Recording cash on delivery as the payment method; the platform itself will not receive any money

## 6. Not Included in the First Version
- **Online payment:** handling real money brings security and legal requirements that are outside the scope of the first version.
- **Delivery and cargo tracking:** delivery will be arranged between the farmer and the consumer.
- **Mobile application:** the web interface will be designed to work on mobile browsers instead.
- **AI-based price prediction:** this requires historical price data and can be added later.

These features are planned as future work and can be developed in the next semester.
